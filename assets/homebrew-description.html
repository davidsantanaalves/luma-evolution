A native OpenGL launcher for LG webOS TVs.

  • Organize favorites in the Evolution Home Dock, launch apps and inputs, and browse your app library.
  • Upload custom backgrounds, toggle gradient shading, and enable automatic background rotation.
  • Customize input names and visibility, with live cable and signal status.
  • Add webhook shortcuts for automations with the QR-code editor.
  • Personalize with greeting, date, clock, and weather.
  • Optional automatic updates for eligible Homebrew apps. Launcher updates are unavailable during the foundation.
  • Show built-in LG AirPlay as an input with visibility and rename controls.
  • Switch running apps and close one or all, with protected inputs and preload-aware filtering.

Available in Czech, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Swedish and Vietnamese.
